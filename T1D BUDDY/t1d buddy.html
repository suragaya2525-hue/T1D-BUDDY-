Learn about Type 1 diabetes

What is Type 1 diabetes?

Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition in which the body produces little or no insulin. It is not caused by eating too much sugar, and it is nobody's fault.

Why is insulin needed?

Insulin helps glucose move from the blood into body cells. Use insulin only according to your prescribed care plan.

The 4–3–2–1–0 reminder

Use this as a personal reminder only if your care team has explained and approved what it means for you. It is not a universal insulin-dosing rule.

When is emergency help needed?

If a person is unconscious, having a seizure, or cannot swallow safely, get emergency medical help immediately. Do not give food or drink by mouth.
